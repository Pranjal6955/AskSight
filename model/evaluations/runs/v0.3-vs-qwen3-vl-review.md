# v0.3 vs Qwen3-VL-2B comparison

Evaluation date: 19 Nov 2026  
Set: same fixed 26 AskSight cases (18 direct questions, 8 photo descriptions)  
Qwen model: `Qwen/Qwen3-VL-2B-Instruct`; saved run did not record a pinned model revision or accelerator name.

## Decision

Do not switch the app to Qwen3-VL based on this comparison. Keep v0.3 for now, and treat both models as prototypes. v0.3 scores higher on the strict direct-answer metric and is much faster in the recorded run. Qwen gives richer photo descriptions, but the sample is small and those descriptions sometimes contain unsupported details. The current evidence does not justify building a production Qwen serving adapter yet.

### Strict VQA accuracy by question category

VQA scoring normalizes punctuation/articles and gives partial credit when an exact normalized answer matches at least 3 of 10 human references. Full sentences are treated as different answers, which disadvantages Qwen's answer style.

| Category | Cases | v0.3 | Qwen3-VL |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Action | 2 | 50.0% | 100.0% |
| Color | 1 | 100.0% | 0.0% |
| Count | 5 | 40.0% | 20.0% |
| Object | 1 | 0.0% | 0.0% |
| Reading text | 1 | 0.0% | 100.0% |
| Scene | 5 | 73.3% | 33.3% |
| Yes/no | 3 | 66.7% | 44.4% |

These are tiny category samples, not stable estimates. For example, color has only one question: v0.3 says `red`, while Qwen says `The truck is red.` The strict score marks Qwen wrong despite the correct color being present.

## Description review

Manual review against the saved expected-fact notes (8 examples; not an independently blinded human study):

| Photo | v0.3 | Qwen3-VL | Review |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dog at table | “A dog is eating a cake with a fork.” | Describes a light-brown dog at a table with a cupcake; guesses a dachshund and adds background details. | Qwen covers more visible details; both risk an incorrect action or unsupported specificity. |
| Winter truck | Calls the subject a snowmobile. | Correctly identifies a red fire truck, snow, and bare trees; adds a claim that it is in disrepair. | Clear Qwen win on main subject and scene facts. |
| Motorcycle street | Correctly identifies a person riding a motorcycle. | Adds clothing, motorcycle details, a blue car, storefronts, and signs. | Qwen is more useful, but its extra specifics need image-level checking. |
| Lakeside park | Invents people sitting on a bench. | Mentions water, lawn, trees, benches, boats, and sky; adds small structures. | Qwen captures more of the scene; its added details need checking. |
| Zebras | Correctly identifies zebras in a field. | Correctly describes zebras and grass; adds fence details. | Both identify the main scene; Qwen is fuller. |
| Yellow pedestrian sign | Misidentifies it as a stop sign. | Correctly reads “LOOK BOTH WAYS” and describes the yellow sign. | Strong Qwen win, including readable text. |
| Kitchen | Vaguely says people are standing around a table. | Describes the kitchen, people, fruit, appliances; invents specific actions and people. | Qwen provides more useful detail but also more unsupported claims. |
| Night harbor | Identifies boats, omitting night and moon. | Mentions night, moon, and boats; guesses the flag is Canadian. | Qwen covers more key facts but makes an unsupported flag identification. |

Qwen's added details are not automatically correct simply because the descriptions are longer. Both systems need testing for omissions and unsupported claims on a larger, representative set of consented photos.

## Recommended code next step

Before adding a new backend model provider, improve the evaluation set: include several hundred consented AskSight-style photos, label expected facts and risky failure types, and score answer correctness separately from answer wording. Then rerun both models with pinned revisions and the same hardware. The existing 26-case sample is useful for finding failure modes, but too small to make a reliable deployment choice.
